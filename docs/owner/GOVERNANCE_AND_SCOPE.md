## Known facts

ZephyrTrade is a source-available MIT research/demo project attributed in [CITATION.cff](../../CITATION.cff) to **ZephyrTrade contributors**; its Python package also declares that contributor attribution. The GitHub owner hosts the code; this does not establish legal ownership of external datasets, images, trading records or third-party services. The [LICENSE](../../LICENSE), source notices and [security policy](../../SECURITY.md) remain authoritative.

## Proposed responsibilities, not assigned roles

| Decision | Accountable reviewer needed before a release |
| --- | --- |
| Change to the settlement/forecast model | Numerical maintainer and domain reviewer |
| Incorporation of real weather/market data | Data license, vintage, quality, privacy and rights reviewer |
| Releasing static browser material | Repository maintainer plus artifact/release owner |
| Running any publicly accessible service | Security/operations owner, new architecture and threat assessment |
| Claiming trading, revenue or financial fitness | Qualified external domain and regulatory review |

No team roster, SLA, external validation, legal approval, financial return, live-broker account or clinical/safety certification is inferred. The [roadmap](../../ROADMAP.md) contains proposed next steps; no dates are commitments.
