# Community conduct

Treat participants with respect. Critique the work, provide reproducible evidence and assume good intent while remaining clear about harm. Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, impersonation, private-data disclosure and spam are not accepted.

Keep project discussions relevant and consent-based. Do not solicit real medical, financial, identity, child, or emergency records for demo testing. Maintainers may edit/remove harmful content and restrict participation in proportion to the conduct. Reports should use a trusted private route to the repository owner; do not repost sensitive material in public. Decisions should identify the behavior and permit a respectful appeal to the owner.

This is the project's own concise conduct policy; it does not claim adoption of a third-party covenant.
